No, We're Not That Stupid

Ok so I saw this tweet earlier today and while simple I think it encapsulates the ragebait landscape we’re in extremely well.

At a glance this sounds extremely concerning. I mean come on, they’re literally called ‘potato chips’. Normally this sort of thing doesn’t phase me but this was especially stupid so I felt the need to dig a bit deeper, so I found the FORTUNE article mentioned.

Pay attention to the loaded language in the title. It’s a drastic rebrand after a disturbing finding. All the emphasis is put on the 42% statistic even though that’s a tiny part of the article. But hey, we’ve found out that only Lay’s consumers are stupid idiots, not all consumers in general. That’s an improvement.

But wait. This article wasn’t even written for FORTUNE! It was originally published on Morning Brew. Let’s see what that article looks like.

This title fits the content of the article far better. The whole point is that Lays is rebranding to put more emphasis on being natural. All FORTUNE did was repost the article with a worse clickbait title. But one thing is still bugging me. What is the source for that 2021 study? I tried googling Lays surveys conducted in 2021, but all I got was stuff about flavor preferences, so it must be some internal thing. Whatever.

Anyways, here’s the kicker. I found the story on Pepsico’s website (not linked by FORTUNE or MorningBrew mind you). Let’s check that out.

Yeah can’t fit that one on a single page. The whole thing is basically a PR piece describing why and how they’re implementing their redesign. But don’t fret! Our 42% statistic shows up again.

…

42% of people don’t realize that LAYS chips are made with REAL, FARM GROWN potatoes. Now the actual survey still isn’t given but this phrasing implies something FAR more reasonable than almost half the population being mouthbreathers. 42% of people thought that Lays chips were made of some chemical slop designed to taste like potatoes rather than actual potatoes. (Hey, it even ties back into the theme of attempting to rebrand as more natural!)

This little graphic I slapped together in 3 minutes can hopefully help you see how absurd the tweet is now. A tiny remark in the original story, misconstrued in another article, with a clickbait title slapped on top, followed by a tweet uncritically referencing it while somehow giving even less information.

So there you have it. The journey of an unsubstantiated statistic from a generic corporate PR piece to another nugget of ragebait that’ll be used to make fun of [guy i disagree with] for 2 days before swiftly being forgotten.
